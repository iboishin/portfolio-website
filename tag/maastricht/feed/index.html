]]> Maastricht is a quaint university town just like Lille. With the gorgeous architecture and the peaceful river, Maastricht should be on everyone’s travel list!

A special thank you to the Maastricht Tourism Office for all of the information.

P.S. If you go during the winter and are looking for a nice warm snack to have while you people-watch, the restaurant outside of the church-turned-library has the most delicious bitterballen and very comfortable winter outside seating.
