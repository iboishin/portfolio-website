Salzburg, Austria

  • Post published: October 23, 2016

Literally translated, Salzburg means salt fortress. Salzburg became a rich town during medieval times thanks to its salt mines and tax on trade that passed through its river. The river separates the city in half, with the “New Town” to the north and the “Old Town” to the south.
A special thanks to the Rick Steves Audio Europe™ Travel App and to Big Boy Travel for the interesting and informative tours!

Note: I apologize for the difference in brightness among my pictures. I visited once in October and later came back in December as I enjoyed the city so much and wanted to learn more.
